Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our medical equipment services, repairs, and maintenance.

1. What types of medical equipment do you service?

We assist with medical equipment that requires repair or maintenance. Please contact us with the equipment's name and the issue so we can determine whether we can accommodate your request.

2. What is Preventive Maintenance Services (PMS)?

Preventive Maintenance Services (PMS) involves regularly checking and maintaining medical equipment to help keep it in good working condition and identify potential problems early.

3. How can I request a repair service?

Yes, You can contact Renz Medical Equipment through the contact information provided on our website. Please include the type of equipment and a brief description of the problem.

4. Do you offer scheduled maintenance?

Yes, You may contact us to inquire about scheduling preventive maintenance for your medical equipment. The schedule will depend on the service requirements and availability.

5. Do you sell medical equipment?

Yes, Please contact us to ask about available medical equipment and current availability.

6. Do you offer refurbished medical equipment?

Yes, You may contact us to inquire about refurbished equipment and its availability.

7. How much do your repair and maintenance services cost?

The cost depends on the type of equipment, the problem, and the service required. Contact us with your equipment details so we can discuss your needs.

8. How long does a repair take?

Repair time depends on the equipment, the nature of the problem, and the parts or work required. We recommend contacting us with the details for an initial assessment.

9. How can I contact Renz Medical Equipment?

You can reach us through the contact details provided on our website to ask about our services, equipment, or other concerns.
